# Notes on a few U.S. cities

## Santa Fe

Santa Fe, New Mexico is the oldest state capital in the United States,
founded in 1610. It sits at roughly 7,000 feet, the highest state capital
by elevation.

## Columbus

Columbus, Ohio is the most populous city in the state and its capital. It
was founded in 1812 at the confluence of the Scioto and Olentangy rivers.

## A note on shared names

Several U.S. cities share a name. "Portland" refers to two distinct places:
the larger Portland in Oregon and the older Portland in Maine. Naming a city
by name alone can be ambiguous, which is exactly the kind of distinction a
grounded system must keep straight rather than silently merge.
