# Amendment No. 2 to the Master Service Agreement

This Amendment No. 2 (the "Amendment") modifies the Master Service Agreement
between Tessera Demonstrations Ltd and Müller Logistik GmbH.

## 1. Change of renewal date

Section 2 of the Agreement is amended as follows: the Agreement auto-renews
annually on 1 February, aligning the contract year with the customer's fiscal
year. The sixty (60) days notice period for termination remains unchanged.

## 2. No other changes

All other terms of the Agreement remain in full force and effect. In the event
of inconsistency between this Amendment and the Agreement, the parties'
intent shall be determined by their authorized representatives.

Signed for Müller Logistik GmbH and Tessera Demonstrations Ltd.
