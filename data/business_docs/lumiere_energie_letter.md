# Letter of Agreement — Billing Arrangement

To: Lumière Énergie
From: Tessera Demonstrations Ltd
Subject: Confirmation of special billing condition

Dear Sir or Madam,

We write to confirm the special condition agreed for your account. By exception
to our standard monthly cycle, invoices to Lumière Énergie will be issued
quarterly, in arrears, consolidating all orders placed within each calendar
quarter into a single statement.

This arrangement is granted for the duration of the current supply relationship
and may be revisited at annual review. A force-majeure clause applies: obligations
are suspended for events beyond reasonable control, including grid-level energy
disruptions.

Kind regards,
Accounts Team
