# Supply Agreement — Terms and Conditions

Supplier: Tessera Demonstrations Ltd. Customer: Bayerische Stahlwerke AG, a steel
manufacturer based in München, Germany.

## 1. Volume discount

The Customer qualifies for a tiered volume discount: 5% on annual order value
above EUR 25,000, rising to 8% above EUR 50,000. Discounts are reconciled at the
end of each calendar year.

## 2. Delivery conditions

Deliveries to the Customer's München works require advance scheduling of at least
five (5) working days, owing to on-site crane availability. Incoterms are DAP
(Delivered At Place) unless otherwise agreed in writing per order.

## 3. Quality and inspection

All steel components are subject to incoming inspection. Non-conforming goods may
be rejected within ten (10) working days of delivery without penalty.
