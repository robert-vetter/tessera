# launch/

Two kinds of material live here, with different visibility:

- **`registries/`** — tracked and public: the MCP-registry submission
  (`server.json` + blurbs + runbook). These are *meant* to be public (the
  registry publishes `server.json`; a test pins it), and contain nothing
  personal.

- **`posts/`, `outreach/`, `sap/`, `zfellows/`** — **local-only, gitignored,
  never published.** The maintainer's own foundation for launching and
  applying: the Show HN / community drafts, the DACH outreach playbook and
  target list, the SAP application kit (cover letters, target teams), and the
  Z Fellows check-in script. They stay on the maintainer's machine and go out
  **only** under his identity, on his timing — they were deliberately removed
  from the public repository before launch (a draft Show HN post, or one's own
  cover letters, do not belong in the public product repo).

Facts inside were verified on the date noted in each file; re-check anything
time-sensitive before sending.
